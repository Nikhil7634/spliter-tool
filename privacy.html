Privacy Policy

Your privacy is our top priority. Learn how we protect your data and images.

Last updated: December 2024

100% Local Processing

Your images never leave your device. All processing happens in your browser.

1. Information We Collect

I Love Image Splitter is designed with privacy-first principles. We do not collect, store, or transmit any of your personal images or sensitive data. Here's what we may collect:

Anonymous Usage Data:

  • Page views and feature usage statistics
  • Browser type and device information
  • Error logs for debugging and improvement

2. Local Processing Technology

All image splitting and processing occurs entirely within your web browser using advanced JavaScript and HTML5 Canvas technology. This means:

What We DO

  • Process images locally in your browser
  • Provide instant results without uploads
  • Ensure complete data privacy
  • Offer offline functionality

What We DON'T DO

  • Upload your images to servers
  • Store your images anywhere
  • Share your content with third parties
  • Track your image content

3. Cookies and Local Storage

We may use cookies and local storage to enhance your experience and remember your preferences:

  • Preference Cookies: Remember your preferred grid sizes and download formats
  • Analytics Cookies: Help us understand how users interact with our tool (anonymous data only)
  • Functional Cookies: Ensure the tool works properly across different browsers

4. Third-Party Services

We may use trusted third-party services to improve our tool and understand user behavior:

  • Analytics Services: Google Analytics or similar for anonymous usage statistics
  • Performance Monitoring: Services to monitor site speed and reliability
  • CDN Services: Content delivery networks for faster loading times

Important: These services never have access to your images or personal content.

5. Data Security

Since your images are processed locally and never transmitted to our servers, they remain completely secure on your device. Additional security measures include:

  • HTTPS Encryption: All connections are secured with SSL/TLS encryption
  • Memory Management: Images are cleared from browser memory after processing
  • Secure Code: Regular security audits and updates to our codebase

6. Your Rights and Choices

You have full control over your privacy when using our tool:

  • Cookie Control: Disable cookies in your browser settings if preferred
  • Private Browsing: Use incognito/private mode for additional privacy
  • Offline Usage: Tool works offline once loaded, no internet required

7. Children's Privacy

Our service is safe for users of all ages. Since we don't collect personal information or store images, there are no special privacy concerns for children. However, we recommend parental supervision for users under 13 years of age when using any online tool.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for other operational, legal, or regulatory reasons. We will notify users of any material changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date.

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or our privacy practices, please don't hesitate to contact us. We're committed to transparency and will respond to your inquiries promptly.
